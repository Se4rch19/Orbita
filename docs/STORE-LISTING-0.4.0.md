Una pequeña luz. Un universo por descubrir.

En Órbita, tu viajero avanza solo. Desliza hacia fuera o hacia el planeta para cambiar de órbita, recoger fragmentos y esquivar peligros que aparecen en tu camino. Cada mundo transforma el ritmo: caminos que se deforman, obstáculos que se desplazan, fracturas y cambios de dirección.

## English — United States

**Name:** Órbita: Worlds in Motion

**Short description:** Swipe between orbits, explore five worlds, and build your planet. Play offline.

**Full description:**

A tiny light. A universe to discover.

In Órbita, your traveler moves on its own. Swipe outward or toward the planet to change orbits, collect fragments, and dodge hazards that appear along your path. Each world brings a different rhythm: shifting paths, drifting obstacles, fractures, and changes of direction.

Luma guides you through a short, hands-on tutorial. Then choose your journey: master the expeditions, reach three goals in the Daily Challenge, explore an endless route, or unwind in Calm.

Fragments and accomplishments earned through play open new possibilities in the Planet Forge. Combine shapes, surfaces, biomes, atmospheres, and space environments. Rotate the preview, save your creation, and play in your own world.

Five visual environments and original music generated on your device accompany every journey. Share challenge codes without creating an account, and keep your discoveries in the Logbook.

Works offline. Available in English and Spanish. This version has no ads or in-app purchases.
